Home

After coming home, there's a very simple thing that happens. I go up to my apartment and unpack my things. And, depending on the time and day of when I've come home, I either go next doors to spend time with my family, ie. my aunt, my cousin, and my other aunt, (And sometimes the occasional uncle and other cousin) or go shower, brush my teeth, go downstairs and make dinner. But that is all dependent on the day and time.

Spending time with my family is nice, as I've gotten a real intimacy I wouldn't normally have, not that the monthly huge family gatherings are too little, I really am blessed to be able to spend that much family time, but it's nice to see a sort of different side. Living with them has really just, I want to say "enlightened" but that would be too much of a joke. If I say enlightened I mean in terms of how much reality TV I now watch when I'm with them. I hang out on the couch doing my homework or other design stuff while The Challenge, Survivor, Big Brother etc goes off in the background. I tried to pay attention, but those people on those shows are really something else.
