Going Home, Really

I commute to school, coming from my grandparents house in Queens, but I also go home to New Jersey mainly to satisfy my mother, as she constantly calls me saying I should come home, something she didn't say last year when I was home ever weekend.

Now, I don't go home every weekend, but when I do, I take the NJ Transit straight after my Friday Class, and go on my way to the train station back home. The first time, I had to walk home, which was way worse than I thought it would be, mainly due to all the uphill walking, my legs were in fact killing me, since there aren't any uphills in the city. When I'm home, I just unpack, pet my dog and let her out. I then just do my homework or play games until my family gets home, and then we eat dinner, catch up, and I sometimes drive my sister wherever she needs to go.
