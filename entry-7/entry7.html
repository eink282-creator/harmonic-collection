Home (But the Weekend)

Now, you may be thinking, why on earth does the entire weekend get it's own section? That's because there's honestly not much different from the rest of my days.

The only real difference is that I just spend more time with my family, that being coming over for breakfast and lunch, before leaving around an hour or so after to do homework, before coming back over for dinner. Then I hang out, watch whatever movie that my cousin is making me watch, which, might I add, ALL OF WHICH SO FAR, HAVE BEEN HORROR MOVIES. Because I am such a big fan of horror movies. That was a joke, I'm not. But, I have since watched, Hereditary, Longlegs, Black Phone, Weapons, Nosfaratu, Abigail, and more that I can't for the life of me remember. And if you say "Oh kai those aren't horror movies" THEY ARE TO ME.
