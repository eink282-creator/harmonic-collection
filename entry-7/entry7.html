Sleep

Sleeping. My relationship with sleep is a complex one.

Having previously suffered from insomnia, having a solid sleep schedule is even more horrendous when given schoolwork. All-nighters are definitly not something I ever previously dealt with, but coming to college definitely made it something I learned I would have to deal with. Albeit, thankfully I don't have to pull all nighters everyday.

But, when I do plan to sleep, there's a good chance I end up just staring at the cieling waiting for sleep to come to me, only for me to be endlessly bothered by little things, like how my necklace is too heavy on my neck, my hair feels too frizzy, or I feel too sweaty under the blankets. And so, I go on my phone. But after like 2 seconds of sitting on my phone, I get super tired, so I put my phone away, only to end up back with the feeling of uncomfortabilty, and so the cycle continues.

But, every so often, I get lucky, and I manage to pass out nicely, forgetting whatever dreams I'll end up having, or remember and write it down in the morning. Granted everytime I sleep nicely, I wake up late.
